"STEPS 1-2 OF BECOMING YOUR OWN BANK:

Traditional banking doesn't work for independent professionals because:
• We have irregular income cycles
• Opportunities require immediate capital
• Banks want steady income documentation
• Access is controlled by someone else's approval

The solution? Create your own banking foundation with these specific elements:
• Protected growth without market risk
• Tax advantages for both accumulation and distribution
• Built-in liquidity for access during contract gaps
• No approval process for accessing your capital

One consultant I worked with used this approach to fund a certification program during a 3-month contract gap, then secured a new contract at 30% higher rates because of the new credential.
