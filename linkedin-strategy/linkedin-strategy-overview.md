# LinkedIn Content Strategy for 7-Step Guide

## Overview

This LinkedIn content strategy is designed to establish thought leadership around the "Become Your Own Bank" concept while driving interest in the 7-step guide lead magnet. The strategy uses a multi-post approach to build engagement and drive lead generation.

## Series Structure

1. **Introduction Post** - Overview of the concept and problem
2. **Core Principles Posts** (3 posts) - Each covers key steps from the guide
3. **Final Call-to-Action Post** - Bringing it all together with an offer

## Content Schedule

| Post | Topic | Timing | Primary Goal |
|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| Post 1 | The Self-Banking Problem | Day 1 | Problem awareness |
| Post 2 | Foundation Building | Day 3-4 | Education |
| Post 3 | Business Application | Day 6-7 | Practical application |
| Post 4 | Building Legacy | Day 9-10 | Long-term vision |
| Post 5 | Call-to-Action | Day 12-14 | Conversion |

## Engagement Strategy

- Respond promptly to all comments, especially those requesting the guide
- Use "GUIDE" comment requests to build social proof
- Consider sharing portions of actual guide content in comment responses
- Tag relevant connections who might find specific posts valuable

## Metrics to Track

- Comments requesting the guide
- Profile views after each post
- Connection requests generated
- Direct messages received
- Guide downloads resulting from LinkedIn strategy

## Visual Strategy

- Use consistent colors and visual style across all posts (navy, gold, white)
- Incorporate professional business imagery with banking concepts
- Create clear visual contrasts between traditional banking and self-banking
- Use professional-quality graphics that align with your brand

## Follow-up Strategy

- Send personalized message to each person who requests the guide
- Follow up one week later with a value-add message asking for feedback
- Invite qualified prospects to a brief call after demonstrating value
- Track guide recipients in CRM for future nurturing
